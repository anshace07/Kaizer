# PlantPulse Demo Plant — Safety Manual (SYNTHETIC)

This manual covers six common incident types on the shop floor. Every worker should know the warning signs, the immediate steps, and who to inform. **When in doubt, treat it as the more serious case and inform the Shift Supervisor.**

Emergency contacts used below (synthetic roles, no real people):
- **Shift Supervisor** — first point of contact for every incident.
- **Maintenance Desk** — mechanical, hydraulic and electrical repairs.
- **Safety Officer** — gas, chemical, fire and any injury.
- **Plant Fire Team** — fire and fire risk.

---

## SOP-101 Smoke / Overheating

**Warning signs**
- Smoke, haze or a burning smell (plastic, rubber, oil) from a machine or panel.
- Motor or bearing housing too hot to touch.
- Discolouration or melting of cables, belts or covers.

**Immediate steps**
1. Stop the machine using the normal stop button if it is safe to reach.
2. Do not open covers or panels while smoke is visible.
3. Keep people away from the area; do not stand in the smoke.
4. If flames appear, follow SOP-103.

**Who to inform**
- Shift Supervisor immediately, then Maintenance Desk.
- Safety Officer if smoke keeps coming after the machine is stopped.

---

## SOP-102 Gas Leak / Chemical Smell

**Warning signs**
- Unusual smell of gas, solvent or chemicals.
- Hissing sound from pipes, valves or tanks.
- Eye or throat irritation, headache or dizziness among workers.
- Gas detector alarm.

**Immediate steps**
1. Do not switch any electrical equipment on or off near the leak.
2. No mobile phone calls, smoking or flames in the area.
3. Move upwind and out of the area; help others leave.
4. Close the isolation valve only if it is far from the leak and you are trained to do so.
5. Anyone feeling unwell goes to fresh air and reports to the first-aid room.

**Who to inform**
- Safety Officer and Shift Supervisor immediately, from a safe location.

---

## SOP-103 Electrical Sparks / Fire

**Warning signs**
- Sparks, flashes or arcing from cables, panels or motors.
- Crackling or buzzing sound from electrical equipment.
- Visible flame or glowing material.

**Immediate steps**
1. Raise the alarm and shout to warn people nearby.
2. Never use water on electrical equipment.
3. Only if trained and the fire is small: use a CO2 extinguisher. Otherwise leave.
4. Evacuate by the nearest marked exit and go to the assembly point.
5. Do not re-enter until the Plant Fire Team clears the area.

**Who to inform**
- Plant Fire Team and Shift Supervisor immediately.
- Safety Officer and Maintenance Desk (Electrical).

## SOP-104 Abnormal Vibration / Noise

**Warning signs**
- Shaking, rattling or knocking that is new or louder than usual.
- Grinding, screeching or metallic sounds.
- Loose bolts, guards or covers.

**Immediate steps**
1. Reduce load or stop the machine if the vibration is strong or getting worse.
2. Keep hands away from moving parts; do not try to tighten anything while running.
3. Note when it started and what the machine was doing.

**Who to inform**
- Maintenance Desk (Mechanical).
- Shift Supervisor if the machine has to be stopped.

---

## SOP-105 Oil / Fluid Leak

**Warning signs**
- Oil, coolant, hydraulic fluid or water on the floor or dripping from the machine.
- Falling level in a sight glass or reservoir.
- Slow or jerky movement in hydraulic machines.

**Immediate steps**
1. Put a warning sign or cone around the spill to prevent slips.
2. Use the spill kit to contain it; do not wash oil into drains.
3. If the leak is from a high-pressure hose, stay clear and stop the machine.
4. If the fluid is a chemical, follow SOP-102.

**Who to inform**
- Maintenance Desk (Hydraulics or Mechanical).
- Shift Supervisor if the machine is stopped or the spill is large.

---

## SOP-106 High Temperature

**Warning signs**
- Temperature reading above the normal range on a gauge or display.
- Overheat alarm or repeated trips.
- Hot air, heat haze or very hot casings near furnaces, ovens or motors.

**Immediate steps**
1. Do not touch hot surfaces; wear heat-resistant gloves if work is needed nearby.
2. Check that cooling (fans, water, coolant) is running, without opening guards.
3. Reduce load or stop the machine if the temperature keeps rising.
4. If there is smoke, follow SOP-101.

**Who to inform**
- Maintenance Desk and Shift Supervisor.
- Safety Officer if a worker feels heat stress (dizziness, heavy sweating, cramps).
